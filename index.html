WHO WE ARE

A humanitarian and development organization rooted in faith and community.

VIRD Foundation is a humanitarian and development organization committed to improving the lives of vulnerable individuals and communities through integrated humanitarian assistance, sustainable development, Islamic social investment and community transformation.

Our Vision

A resilient and empowered society where vulnerable people live with dignity, opportunity and sustainable livelihoods.

Our Mission

To improve the lives of vulnerable individuals and communities through accountable humanitarian assistance, integrated development, Islamic social investment, resilience building and sustainable community transformation.

SAFEGUARDING

Protecting People. Preventing Harm.

VIRD Foundation maintains a survivor-centred approach to safeguarding across every programme and partnership.

  • Child safeguarding
  • Protection from sexual exploitation and abuse
  • Prevention of sexual harassment
  • Code of conduct
  • Safe recruitment
  • Staff training
  • Incident reporting
  • Confidential complaints mechanisms
